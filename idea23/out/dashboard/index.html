Does reasoning help the reader?

Ablation on multi-page questions at five pages: the same reader asked to reason step by step and end with "Answer: …" (up to 512 tokens), against the short-answer prompt used everywhere else.

How many needs the decomposer finds

Qwen3-8B splits each multi-page question into at most four needs. With one need, coverage nearly reduces to top-k (the need's score only reorders the first picks), so its gains come where m is 2 or more.

Pipeline

MMLongBench-Doc, answerable questions with valid labels and not on the two mismatched PDFs. Nothing is trained or tuned, so every question is test data. Multi-page: 2 to 5 evidence pages; control: one evidence page.

retriever
ColQwen2.5 (vidore/colqwen2.5-v0.2), pages at 144 dpi, up to 768 visual tokens; MaxSim score s(x, p) for a query x and page p.
decomposer
Qwen3-8B, no thinking, hand-written few-shot examples not from the benchmark; at most 4 needs per question.
reader
Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct, selected pages in page order with their page numbers, up to 1,024 visual tokens per page, short answer in the expected format.
scoring
idea5/score.py, the rule-based part of the benchmark's scorer; correct when the score is above 0.
page tags
for cross-modal questions, Qwen2.5-VL-7B picks which of the question's labelled modalities each gold page holds; single-modality questions take their label.

Caveats: a few benchmark answer-format labels are wrong (for example a ratio question labelled List), which the scorer follows; the question types are a keyword heuristic; and the partition / composition categories of Analysis 2 cover only 17 of these questions, so they are reported in out/analysis/summary.md and not here.

Selectors

s^ is the score min-max normalised over the pages of the document. Rerankers choose from the same candidate pool, the ColQwen top 20.

topk
top k pages by s(q, p), as in M3DocRAG.
bm25
top k by BM25 over the PDF text layer.
hybrid
top k by the sum of z-scored ColQwen and BM25 scores.
mmr
greedy a s^(q, p) minus (1 - a) times the highest cosine similarity to a selected page, a = 0.5.
decomp_rrf
reciprocal-rank fusion (c = 60) of the rankings of the question and of every need.
coverage
ours, v0: greedy maximisation of F(S) = sum over needs of (1/m) max over S of s^(d_j, p), plus (1/k) times the sum over S of s^(q, p).
coverage_beam
the same objective with beam search, 3 partial sets.
coverage_full
coverage over the whole document instead of the top 20 (retrieval only).
gold
the labelled evidence pages (reader only).
